# LiteraMK-AI – AI Usage Log

## 1. General Information

- **Project Name:** LiteraMK-AI
- **Course:** Introduction to Artificial Intelligence
- **AI Tool Used:** OpenAI ChatGPT
- **Purpose:** AI-assisted learning, development, debugging, evaluation, and documentation.

## 2. Purpose of AI Assistance

ChatGPT was used as a supporting tool throughout the development of LiteraMK-AI.

AI assistance included explanations of artificial intelligence concepts, implementation guidance, code generation, debugging, evaluation methodology, interpretation of results, and drafting project documentation.

The student executed the code, examined the outputs, tested the application, and participated in the development process.

## 5. Limitations of AI Assistance

The following limitations were identified:

- AI-generated code may require debugging or modification.
- AI-generated explanations may contain inaccuracies.
- AI-assisted relevance assessments are subjective.
- Selected text excerpts may not represent the complete themes of literary works.
- AI assistance cannot replace independent literary expertise.
- Generated documentation must be checked against actual implementation results.
